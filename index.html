Grooming, done the way you ask for it

Tell us the cut you want and we'll talk it through with you before we start, especially on anything close to the skin. We groom dogs and cats, matted coats included, and we're patient with nervous pets.

What's on our shelves

Most of our foods skip corn, wheat, soy and preservatives. Beyond food we carry toys, collars, bowls, leashes, tie outs, treats, clothing, small animal supplies, and engravable pet tags in several styles.

Asking about a puppy

Our puppies are hand selected from private breeders and checked by a vet before they come into the store. They're up to date on shots and worming, and come with a 20 day health guarantee and a 2 year warranty covering genetic and hereditary defects. Call the store to ask what's available.
